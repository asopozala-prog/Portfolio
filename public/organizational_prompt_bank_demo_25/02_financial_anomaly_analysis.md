# 02 — Financial anomaly analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Excel`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Validation`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Financial anomaly analysis`

---

Analyze the selected financial data for unusual patterns that deserve review.

## Goal
Identify anomalies that may affect financial interpretation or follow-up.

## Analyze
- Look for unexpected changes, outliers, duplicates, gaps, or inconsistent totals.
- Compare relevant periods or categories where the workbook supports it.
- Separate likely data-quality issues from potentially meaningful business changes.

## Context
Use the selected Excel data and its visible labels, formulas, and time periods.

## Output
Summarize the most important anomalies, their location, and why each matters.

## Check
Validate observations against the available data. Mark uncertainty clearly and do not infer causes without evidence.
