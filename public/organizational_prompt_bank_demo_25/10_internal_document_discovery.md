# 10 — Internal document discovery

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Drive`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Classification`, `Extraction`  
**Task:** `Internal document discovery`

---

Find the internal documents most relevant to the working question I provide.

## Goal
Reduce manual searching and surface the strongest internal sources.

## Search
- Interpret the task and important concepts.
- Identify documents that directly address the question.
- Extract enough context to explain why each result is relevant.

## Context
Search only the Google Drive content available to me and respect existing access permissions.

## Output
Return a ranked shortlist with document title, relevance, and the useful information found.

## Check
Do not present weak matches as authoritative. Note when the available documents do not answer the question.
