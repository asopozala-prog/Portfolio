# 14 — Long policy review

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Document Analysis`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Extraction`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Long policy review`

---

Review the supplied long policy document and surface what a responsible owner needs to know.

## Goal
Make a complex policy easier to understand and review.

## Review
- Extract major obligations, responsibilities, exceptions, and controls.
- Identify sections with ambiguity, duplication, or internal tension.
- Summarize material changes or dependencies when visible in the document.

## Context
Use the supplied policy document as the source of truth.

## Output
Return a concise policy overview followed by the most important review points.

## Check
Quote or reference the relevant section for significant concerns and do not invent legal interpretation.
