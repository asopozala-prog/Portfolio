# 03 — Meeting decision extraction

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Teams`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Meeting decision extraction`

---

Review the selected meeting transcript or notes and extract the decisions that matter.

## Goal
Turn the meeting record into a concise decision summary.

## Extract
- Confirmed decisions and approvals.
- Assigned actions, owners, and deadlines.
- Open questions that still require a decision.

## Context
Use only information stated in the Teams meeting material. Do not treat suggestions as approved decisions.

## Output
Provide a short meeting summary followed by Decisions, Actions, and Open Questions.

## Check
Flag any decision whose status, owner, or deadline is ambiguous.
