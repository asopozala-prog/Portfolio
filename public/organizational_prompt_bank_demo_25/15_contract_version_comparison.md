# 15 — Contract version comparison

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Document Analysis`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Extraction`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Contract version comparison`

---

Compare the supplied contract versions and identify material differences.

## Goal
Help a reviewer understand what changed without manually comparing every paragraph.

## Compare
- Identify additions, removals, and changed obligations.
- Highlight changes to dates, amounts, responsibilities, termination, or approvals.
- Separate substantive changes from wording-only edits.

## Context
Use only the contract versions supplied.

## Output
Provide a structured change summary with references to the affected clauses or sections.

## Check
Do not give legal advice or infer intent. Flag changes whose significance requires specialist review.
