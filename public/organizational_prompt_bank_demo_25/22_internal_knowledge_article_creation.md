# 22 — Internal knowledge article creation

**Executor Type:** `Knowledge AI`  
**Executor:** `Notion AI`  
**Environment:** `Knowledge Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Internal knowledge article creation`

---

Turn the supplied internal material into a reusable knowledge article.

## Goal
Help colleagues understand and apply the information without reading all source notes.

## Build
- Identify the central process, concept, or answer.
- Remove repetition and organize information logically.
- Preserve important steps, conditions, ownership, and exceptions.

## Context
Use only the selected workspace material and existing terminology.

## Output
Create a concise article with title, overview, key guidance, and related notes where needed.

## Check
Flag outdated, conflicting, or incomplete source information instead of silently resolving it.
