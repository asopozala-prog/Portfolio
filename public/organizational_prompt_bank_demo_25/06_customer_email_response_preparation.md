# 06 — Customer email response preparation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Gmail`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Customer email response preparation`

---

Prepare a professional response to the selected customer email.

## Goal
Answer the customer clearly while protecting accuracy and the existing relationship.

## Prepare
- Identify the customer’s main request and tone.
- Address each question or requested action.
- Keep commitments aligned with information available in the email thread.

## Context
Use the selected Gmail conversation as the factual basis. Preserve relevant names, dates, and references.

## Output
Draft one concise email response suitable for review before sending.

## Check
Do not invent prices, deadlines, policies, or promises. Flag anything that requires internal confirmation.
