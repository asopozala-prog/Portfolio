# 20 — Sales opportunity prioritization

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce`  
**Environment:** `CRM`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Sales opportunity prioritization`

---

Prioritize the selected sales opportunities using the evidence available in CRM.

## Goal
Help the team focus attention on opportunities with the strongest current case for action.

## Assess
- Review stage, value, timing, engagement, and known blockers.
- Classify opportunities by practical priority.
- Explain the strongest evidence behind each priority.

## Context
Use only the available Salesforce opportunity and account information.

## Output
Return a ranked shortlist with priority level, rationale, and recommended next action.

## Check
Do not treat missing CRM data as positive evidence. Flag records that need updating before reliable prioritization.
