# 21 — Support case triage

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce`  
**Environment:** `Customer Service`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Extraction`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Support case triage`

---

Triage the selected customer support cases for appropriate handling.

## Goal
Help the service team identify urgency, issue type, and next action quickly.

## Triage
- Extract the customer’s core issue and relevant context.
- Classify issue type and practical urgency.
- Identify stated deadlines, impact, previous actions, and escalation signals.

## Context
Use the case record and available customer history.

## Output
Return a concise case summary, classification, priority, and recommended routing or next action.

## Check
Do not invent technical causes or service commitments. Flag cases where information is insufficient for confident routing.
