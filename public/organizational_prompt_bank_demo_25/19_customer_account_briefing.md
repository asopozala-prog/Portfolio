# 19 — Customer account briefing

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce`  
**Environment:** `CRM`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Customer account briefing`

---

Prepare a concise briefing for the selected customer account.

## Goal
Help the account owner understand the relationship before the next interaction.

## Review
- Summarize account status and recent activity.
- Surface open opportunities, issues, commitments, and important contacts.
- Identify signals that may require follow-up or preparation.

## Context
Use the CRM information available for the selected account and respect record permissions.

## Output
Return a short account overview, current priorities, and suggested preparation points.

## Check
Distinguish recorded facts from recommendations and flag stale or incomplete account information.
