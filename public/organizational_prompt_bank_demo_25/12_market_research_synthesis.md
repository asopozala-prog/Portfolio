# 12 — Market research synthesis

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Research Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Summarization`, `Analysis`  
**Task:** `Market research synthesis`

---

Synthesize the supplied market research into a decision-oriented overview.

## Goal
Explain the market signals that are most relevant to the business question.

## Synthesize
- Identify major trends, competitors, customer signals, and market changes.
- Separate recurring evidence from isolated observations.
- Highlight implications that are supported by the research.

## Context
Use the research sources supplied or available within this workspace for the task.

## Output
Return an executive summary, key findings, and a short implications section.

## Check
Distinguish source facts from interpretation and flag important gaps in the evidence.
