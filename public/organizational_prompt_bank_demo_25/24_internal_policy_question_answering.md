# 24 — Internal policy question answering

**Executor Type:** `Custom Organizational AI`  
**Executor:** `Internal RAG Agent`  
**Environment:** `Internal Knowledge Base`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`, `Finance & Administration`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Extraction`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Internal policy question answering`

---

Answer my internal policy question using the approved organizational knowledge base.

## Goal
Give a clear answer that employees can trace back to internal policy.

## Find
- Retrieve the policy material directly relevant to the question.
- Extract applicable rules, conditions, exceptions, and responsibilities.
- Prefer current approved sources over older material.

## Context
Use only information available through the internal knowledge base and within my access rights.

## Output
Answer the question concisely and cite the relevant internal source or policy section.

## Check
If the knowledge base does not support a reliable answer, say so and identify what needs confirmation.
