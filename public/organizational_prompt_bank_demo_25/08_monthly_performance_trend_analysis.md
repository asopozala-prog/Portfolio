# 08 — Monthly performance trend analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Sheets`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Monthly performance trend analysis`

---

Analyze the supplied monthly performance data and explain the trends that matter for management.

## Goal
Identify meaningful changes and where management attention may be needed.

## Analyze
- Compare current performance with relevant previous periods.
- Identify improving, declining, and unusually volatile measures.
- Distinguish material changes from minor variation.

## Context
Use only the data and definitions available in the selected Google Sheets material.

## Output
Summarize the main trends, supporting figures, and the most important management implications.

## Check
Do not infer causes that the data cannot support. Flag missing or inconsistent data that affects interpretation.
