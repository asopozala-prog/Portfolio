# 05 — Executive presentation creation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `PowerPoint`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Executive presentation creation`

---

Turn the supplied business material into a concise executive presentation.

## Goal
Help senior leaders understand the situation, implications, and required decisions quickly.

## Build
- Lead with the central message.
- Select only evidence that supports the management discussion.
- Separate facts, implications, recommendations, and decisions required.

## Context
Use the provided PowerPoint content and connected working material available for this task.

## Output
Create a short slide structure with slide title, key message, and essential supporting points.

## Check
Avoid unsupported claims and unnecessary detail. Flag missing evidence behind important recommendations.
