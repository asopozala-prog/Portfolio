# 13 — Business scenario evaluation

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Analysis Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Business scenario evaluation`

---

Evaluate the business scenarios provided and compare their practical implications.

## Goal
Support a management decision without hiding trade-offs.

## Evaluate
- Compare benefits, costs, risks, dependencies, and reversibility.
- Identify assumptions that materially affect each scenario.
- Note where one option is stronger under specific conditions.

## Context
Use the supplied scenario information and business constraints only.

## Output
Provide a concise comparison and a reasoned recommendation when the evidence supports one.

## Check
Do not manufacture missing financial or operational evidence. Make uncertain assumptions explicit.
