# 16 — Research dossier synthesis

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Research Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Research dossier synthesis`

---

Synthesize the supplied research dossier into a coherent evidence-based briefing.

## Goal
Turn a large research collection into a usable management view.

## Synthesize
- Identify the strongest findings and recurring themes.
- Group evidence around the central research question.
- Surface disagreements, limitations, and unresolved questions.

## Context
Use the supplied research materials and preserve distinctions between sources.

## Output
Return an executive summary, thematic findings, and key uncertainties.

## Check
Do not overstate weak evidence. Clearly distinguish documented findings from your synthesis.
