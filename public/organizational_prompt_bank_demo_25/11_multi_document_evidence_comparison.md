# 11 — Multi-document evidence comparison

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `File Analysis`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Extraction`, `Analysis`  
**Task:** `Multi-document evidence comparison`

---

Compare the supplied documents and identify where their evidence agrees or differs.

## Goal
Create a reliable cross-document view without losing source distinctions.

## Compare
- Extract claims, figures, dates, and decisions relevant to the question.
- Identify agreements, contradictions, and missing evidence.
- Keep each finding traceable to its source document.

## Context
Use only the files supplied for this analysis.

## Output
Provide a concise comparison organized around the most important issues, with source references.

## Check
Do not reconcile contradictions by guessing. Clearly mark uncertainty and unsupported conclusions.
