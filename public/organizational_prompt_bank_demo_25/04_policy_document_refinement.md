# 04 — Policy document refinement

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Word`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Transformation`, `Validation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Policy document refinement`

---

Refine the selected policy document while preserving its intended rules and meaning.

## Goal
Make the policy clearer, more consistent, and easier for employees to use.

## Refine
- Simplify unclear or repetitive wording.
- Standardize headings, terms, and instruction style.
- Preserve obligations, exceptions, responsibilities, and approval requirements.

## Context
Use the existing Word document as the source of truth. Do not introduce new policy requirements.

## Output
Return the revised policy text in a clear professional structure.

## Check
Identify passages that appear contradictory, incomplete, or risky to rewrite without owner confirmation.
