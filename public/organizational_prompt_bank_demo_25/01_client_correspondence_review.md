# 01 — Client correspondence review

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Outlook`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Extraction`, `Classification`  
**Task:** `Client correspondence review`

---

Review the selected client correspondence and produce a concise working brief.

## Goal
Help me understand the exchange quickly and identify what needs attention.

## Review
- Summarize the client’s main request or concern.
- Extract commitments, dates, amounts, documents, and named actions.
- Classify each open item as information, action, decision, or risk.

## Context
Use only the correspondence available in this Outlook thread or selection. Distinguish client statements from internal statements.

## Output
Return a short summary, followed by open items with owner and deadline when stated.

## Check
Flag unclear, conflicting, or missing information. Do not invent facts or commitments.
