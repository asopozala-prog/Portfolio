# 09 — Management briefing deck preparation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Slides`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Generation`, `Transformation`  
**Task:** `Management briefing deck preparation`

---

Prepare a concise management briefing from the supplied business material.

## Goal
Give decision-makers a fast, structured view of the topic.

## Structure
- Establish the situation and why it matters.
- Select the strongest supporting evidence.
- Surface risks, options, and decisions required where relevant.

## Context
Use the provided Google Slides content and source material available for the task.

## Output
Create a compact slide-by-slide briefing outline with one key message per slide.

## Check
Keep facts separate from recommendations and flag important information that is missing or uncertain.
