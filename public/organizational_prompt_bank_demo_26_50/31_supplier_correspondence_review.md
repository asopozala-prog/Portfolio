# 31 — Supplier correspondence review

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Gmail`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Extraction`, `Classification`  
**Task:** `Supplier correspondence review`

---

## Goal
Turn the supplied material into a concise working summary.

## Work
- Identify the central message and most important details.
- Remove repetition while preserving relevant context.
- Surface actions, risks, or open questions when present.

## Context
Use only information available in the current `Gmail` task context and respect existing organizational access boundaries.

## Output
Return a concise result designed for human review, emphasizing the information most relevant to **supplier correspondence review**.

## Check
Do not invent missing facts. Flag contradictions, uncertainty, or missing information that could materially affect the result.
