Find and synthesize the information most relevant to the working question.

- Focus on sources directly related to the task.
- Extract the strongest useful evidence.
- Distinguish established information from uncertainty.

## Context
Use only information available in the current `Knowledge Work` task context and respect existing organizational access boundaries.

## Output
Return a concise result designed for human review, emphasizing the information most relevant to **research findings consolidation**.

## Check
