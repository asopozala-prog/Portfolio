# 46 — Internal process documentation

**Executor Type:** `Productivity / Knowledge AI`  
**Executor:** `Notion AI`  
**Environment:** `Documentation`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Internal process documentation`

---

## Goal
Turn the supplied material into a concise working summary.

## Work
- Identify the central message and most important details.
- Remove repetition while preserving relevant context.
- Surface actions, risks, or open questions when present.

## Context
Use only information available in the current `Documentation` task context and respect existing organizational access boundaries.

## Output
Return a concise result designed for human review, emphasizing the information most relevant to **internal process documentation**.

## Check
Do not invent missing facts. Flag contradictions, uncertainty, or missing information that could materially affect the result.
