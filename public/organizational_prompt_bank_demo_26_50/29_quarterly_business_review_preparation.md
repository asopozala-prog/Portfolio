# 29 — Quarterly business review preparation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `PowerPoint`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Analysis`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Quarterly business review preparation`

---

## Goal
Turn the supplied material into a concise working summary.

## Work
- Identify the central message and most important details.
- Remove repetition while preserving relevant context.
- Surface actions, risks, or open questions when present.

## Context
Use only information available in the current `PowerPoint` task context and respect existing organizational access boundaries.

## Output
Return a concise result designed for human review, emphasizing the information most relevant to **quarterly business review preparation**.

## Check
Do not invent missing facts. Flag contradictions, uncertainty, or missing information that could materially affect the result.
