# 76 — Procedure document gap review

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Word`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Procedure document gap review`

---

## Review Question
What is missing from this procedure that could cause people to execute it inconsistently?

## Inspect
Check whether the document defines trigger, prerequisites, sequence, owners, handoffs, approvals, required records, exceptions, and completion criteria.

## Gap Types
Classify findings as Missing Step, Missing Owner, Missing Control, Ambiguous Instruction, Broken Reference, or Undefined Exception.

## Output
List each gap, where it occurs, its operational consequence, and what clarification is needed.

## Rule
Do not invent the missing procedure. The goal is to identify gaps for the process owner, not complete the process yourself.
