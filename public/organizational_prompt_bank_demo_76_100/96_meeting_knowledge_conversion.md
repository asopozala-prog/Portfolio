# 96 — Meeting knowledge conversion

**Executor Type:** `Productivity / Knowledge AI`  
**Executor:** `Notion AI`  
**Environment:** `Internal Knowledge`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Meeting knowledge conversion`

---

## Goal
Convert a meeting record into durable internal knowledge.

## Extract
Keep decisions, agreed practices, definitions, reusable explanations, lessons learned, and process changes. Remove scheduling chatter and temporary coordination.

## Transform
Rewrite durable points so they make sense without the original meeting transcript. Preserve context when it affects how the knowledge should be applied.

## Output
Create: Knowledge Created, Decisions, Reusable Guidance, Follow-up Items.

## Governance
Do not turn unresolved discussion into official guidance. Mark items that still need approval or owner confirmation.
