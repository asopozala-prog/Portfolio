# 81 — Recruitment correspondence summary

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Gmail`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Extraction`, `Classification`  
**Task:** `Recruitment correspondence summary`

---

## Purpose
Summarize a recruitment email thread for the hiring team.

## Capture
Identify candidate, vacancy, stage, interview timing, availability, requested documents, salary expectations, notice period, visa/work-status information, and follow-ups where explicitly stated.

## Status
Classify items as Completed, Awaiting Candidate, Awaiting Company, or Decision Needed.

## Output
Write a short candidate-status summary and list the open actions with dates.

## HR Check
Do not infer enthusiasm, rejection, or hiring intent from polite wording. Flag missing attachments, conflicting dates, and unclear next steps.
