# 78 — Correspondence priority assessment

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Outlook`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Correspondence priority assessment`

---

## Goal
Prioritize Outlook correspondence by business importance.

## Signals
Look for explicit deadlines, financial exposure, blocked work, customer or supplier impact, executive decisions, legal or regulatory timing, and unresolved commitments.

## Priority Model
Use Immediate, High, Normal, or Low. Explain the evidence behind each rating and note whether someone else is already handling the issue.

## Output
Return a ranked list with subject, required action, deadline, and priority rationale.

## Guardrail
Do not make senior senders automatically “urgent.” Emotional tone alone is not enough to justify high priority.
