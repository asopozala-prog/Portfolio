# 77 — Expense category analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Excel`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Expense category analysis`

---

## Goal
Understand how expenses are distributed and where classification or spending patterns need review.

## Categorize
Use the workbook’s existing expense categories. Identify uncategorized transactions, inconsistent labels, duplicates, unusually large entries, and potential misclassifications.

## Analyze
Compare category totals across periods and highlight categories with material growth, decline, or concentration.

## Output
Provide: Category Overview, Major Changes, Exceptions, Transactions Needing Review.

## Finance Check
Preserve original amounts and periods. Do not reclassify ambiguous transactions as fact; mark them for finance confirmation.
