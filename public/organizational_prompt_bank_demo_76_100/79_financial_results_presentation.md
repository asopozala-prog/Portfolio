# 79 — Financial results presentation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `PowerPoint`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Financial results presentation`

---

## Presentation Goal
Turn the supplied financial results into a short leadership presentation.

## Select
Choose the few metrics that explain performance: revenue, cost, margin, cash, variance to plan, or other measures actually present in the source.

## Tell the Story
Lead with overall result, then explain major drivers, risks, and outlook. Keep detailed tables behind the main message.

## Output
Create a slide sequence with headline, supporting figures, and management implication.

## Accuracy
Preserve units, periods, and definitions. Do not invent causes, forecasts, or management commentary absent from the source.
