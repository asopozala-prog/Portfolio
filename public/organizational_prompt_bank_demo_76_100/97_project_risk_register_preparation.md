# 97 — Project risk register preparation

**Executor Type:** `Productivity / Knowledge AI`  
**Executor:** `Notion AI`  
**Environment:** `Project Work`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Classification`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Project risk register preparation`

---

## Task
Prepare a project risk register from existing project material.

## Identify
Find potential events or conditions that could affect scope, schedule, cost, quality, compliance, resources, or delivery. Capture cause, consequence, owner, mitigation, and trigger where stated.

## Separate
Keep future risks apart from issues already happening. Use qualitative likelihood or impact only when evidence supports it.

## Output
Create Risk, Evidence, Impact, Owner, Mitigation, Status.

## Rule
Do not invent probabilities, owners, or mitigations. Flag risks that require project-team assessment.
