# 94 — Customer interaction pattern analysis

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI Platforms`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce / Einstein`  
**Environment:** `CRM`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Customer interaction pattern analysis`

---

## Question
What patterns in customer interactions should the account team pay attention to?

## Analyze
Review interaction frequency, channel, direction, topic, outcome, response gaps, repeated concerns, and changes in engagement over time.

## Distinguish
Separate account-wide patterns from one-off contacts. Note when CRM activity is incomplete or stale before interpreting silence.

## Output
Provide Pattern, Evidence, Possible Significance, and Suggested Next Action.

## Guardrail
Do not infer customer intent, satisfaction, or churn from missing activity alone. Keep recommendations proportional to recorded evidence.
