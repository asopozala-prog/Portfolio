# 83 — Marketing campaign performance analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Sheets`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Marketing campaign performance analysis`

---

## Business Question
Which marketing activity is actually performing well, and where is budget being wasted?

## Analyze
Use available spend, impressions, clicks, leads, conversions, revenue, engagement, CAC, ROAS, or other metrics present in the sheet. Compare campaigns, channels, audiences, and periods.

## Interpret
Separate scale from efficiency. Note tracking gaps, attribution limits, and small samples before drawing conclusions.

## Output
Provide Winners, Underperformers, Key Drivers, and Recommended Next Questions.

## Check
Do not claim causality from correlation. Calculate ratios only when numerator and denominator are available and comparable.
