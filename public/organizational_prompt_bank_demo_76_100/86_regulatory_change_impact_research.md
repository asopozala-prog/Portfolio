# 86 — Regulatory change impact research

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Research`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Regulatory change impact research`

---

## Research Goal
Understand what a regulatory change means for the organization.

## Establish
Identify the official change, effective date, affected entities, transition period, reporting duties, controls, or operational obligations from authoritative sources.

## Impact
Map confirmed requirements to business processes, finance, reporting, systems, contracts, or governance where the task provides enough context.

## Output
Provide: What Changed, When It Applies, Potential Organizational Impact, Questions for Specialist Review.

## Boundary
Do not give legal advice or assume applicability. Cite source dates and clearly separate regulatory text from interpretation.
