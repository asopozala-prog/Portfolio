# 80 — Meeting topic and commitment tracking

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Teams`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Classification`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Meeting topic and commitment tracking`

---

## Task
Track meeting topics and the commitments attached to them.

## Extract
Identify each substantive topic, then capture decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, and dependencies stated during that discussion.

## Commitment Status
Use Confirmed, Proposed, Pending, or Unclear. Do not assign a commitment to someone unless the meeting explicitly does so.

## Output
Create a topic summary followed by a commitment tracker with owner, due date, and status.

## Check
Flag contradictory statements and vague commitments such as “we should” or “someone needs to” rather than treating them as assigned actions.
