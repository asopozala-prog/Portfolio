# 91 — Large knowledge collection synthesis

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`  
**Environment:** `Knowledge Work`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Classification`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Large knowledge collection synthesis`

---

## Challenge
Turn a large knowledge collection into a structure people can actually navigate.

## Organize
Identify major themes, recurring questions, core processes, authoritative guidance, supporting material, duplicates, outdated content, and contradictions.

## Synthesize
For each theme, explain the key knowledge and show which sources are most authoritative or most current.

## Output
Create: Collection Overview, Theme Map, Key Sources, Known Contradictions, Knowledge Gaps.

## Governance
Do not merge conflicting sources into a single “clean” answer. Preserve provenance and uncertainty.
