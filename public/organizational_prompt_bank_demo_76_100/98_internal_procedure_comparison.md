# 98 — Internal procedure comparison

**Executor Type:** `Custom Organizational AI`  
**Executor:** `Internal RAG Agent`  
**Environment:** `Proprietary Knowledge Base`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Comparison`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Internal procedure comparison`

---

## Purpose
Compare internal procedures to find where teams are instructed to work differently.

## Retrieve
Locate the relevant current procedures and extract steps, owners, approvals, controls, exceptions, records, versions, and source authority.

## Compare
Identify aligned steps, conflicting instructions, process variations by scope, and differences caused by version or department.

## Output
Present the comparison by process stage with source references and a list of issues needing clarification.

## Validation
Do not assume the newest-looking document is authoritative. Flag conflicts between approved procedures instead of resolving them yourself.
