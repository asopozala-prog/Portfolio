# 100 — Confidential agreement comparison

**Executor Type:** `Local / Private AI`  
**Executor:** `Local LLM System`  
**Environment:** `Private Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Extraction`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Confidential agreement comparison`

---

## Review Goal
Compare two confidential agreements and show what materially changed.

## Compare
Review parties, scope, dates, price/payment, responsibilities, approvals, confidentiality, liability-related wording where present, termination, renewal, and operational obligations.

## Significance
Separate cosmetic wording changes from changes that could alter cost, timing, control, responsibility, or exposure.

## Output
Create a structured change summary with Clause / Topic, Agreement A, Agreement B, Difference, Review Significance.

## Boundary
Keep processing local. Do not provide legal advice or decide enforceability; flag ambiguous or high-impact clauses for specialist review.
