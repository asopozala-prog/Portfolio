# 90 — Multiple stakeholder position comparison

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`  
**Environment:** `Documents`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Comparison`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Multiple stakeholder position comparison`

---

## Purpose
Compare how different stakeholders position themselves on the same issue.

## Extract
For each stakeholder capture stated objective, preferred option, concerns, conditions, red lines, proposed actions, and any change of position over time.

## Compare
Show areas of alignment, direct disagreement, negotiable space, and dependencies between positions.

## Output
Provide one concise profile per stakeholder, then summarize Alignment, Conflict, and Open Questions.

## Caution
Do not infer motives, politics, or hidden interests. Use only explicit statements from the source documents.
