# 89 — Document chronology reconstruction

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`  
**Environment:** `Documents`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Classification`, `Analysis`  
**Task:** `Document chronology reconstruction`

---

## Objective
Reconstruct a reliable timeline from several documents.

## Extract
Capture dated communications, approvals, transactions, decisions, submissions, revisions, and other relevant events. Record the source for each event.

## Time Logic
Distinguish document date from event date. Keep approximate dates, conflicting dates, and undated events visibly separate.

## Output
Create a chronology with Date, Event, Parties, Source, and Why It Matters.

## Rule
Do not resolve contradictions by guessing. Highlight gaps where the sequence cannot be established from the documents.
