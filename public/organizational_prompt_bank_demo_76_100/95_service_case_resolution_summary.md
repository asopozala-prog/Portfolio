# 95 — Service case resolution summary

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI Platforms`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce / Einstein`  
**Environment:** `Service`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Extraction`, `Transformation`  
**Task:** `Service case resolution summary`

---

## Objective
Create a useful service-case resolution summary for future agents.

## Reconstruct
State the original issue, diagnostics, actions taken, customer communications, workaround, final fix, confirmation of outcome, and remaining follow-up.

## Clean Up
Remove internal chatter and duplicate notes while preserving details needed to understand what solved the case.

## Output
Use: Issue, Investigation, Resolution, Customer Outcome, Follow-up.

## Validation
Do not mark the case resolved unless the record supports it. Flag unresolved symptoms, pending confirmation, or undocumented closure.
