# Mushroom House Berlin II

## Investment Business Plan (Draft)

### Target Launch: 2027

Prepared by Alex Hoffman in cooperation with Thomas Mariño

## Executive Summary

Mushroom House Berlin has operated continuously since 2009 as a
specialized premium mushroom supply business serving Berlin's gastronomy
sector and curated private retail. The proposed 2027 investment
establishes a second operational hub that separates logistics, inventory
intelligence, customer communication and digital operations while
preserving Thomas Mariño's grading standards and supplier relationships.

The competitive advantage is operational knowledge accumulated over
nearly two decades.

## Problem

Wild mushrooms are highly seasonal, perishable and geographically
unstable. Restaurants require reliable quality, transparent grading and
flexible substitutions despite uncertain supply.

## Opportunity

The business serves premium Berlin gastronomy with a model built around
freshness, adaptive allocation and trusted supplier relationships rather
than commodity wholesale.

## Solution

Create a second operational hub supported by ML-assisted inventory
intelligence.

Core modules: - Live inventory - Supplier intelligence - Pre-order
forecasting - Allocation engine - Mushroom Mood Today retail balancing -
Bookkeeping integration

## Products

### B2B

-   Premium wild mushrooms
-   Premium cultivated mushrooms
-   Seasonal chef allocations
-   Truffle programs

### B2C

-   Seasonal recipe bundles

## Market

Primary market: - Berlin - Potsdam - Brandenburg premium gastronomy

## Business Model

Revenue: 1. B2B supply 2. Premium retail 3. Recipe bundles 4. Truffle

## Operations

Supplier regions: - Mazury (Poland) - Carpathians (Romania) - Baltic
forests (Latvia/Lithuania) - Black Sea / Anatolia (Türkiye)

Flow: Supplier → Inspection → Live Inventory → Pre-orders → Allocation →
Delivery → Retail balancing.

## Technology

ML assists: - inventory prediction - pricing - bookkeeping -
communication - homepage updates

Thomas remains responsible for grading and purchasing.

## Marketing

Relationship-first marketing: - chefs - newsletters - supplier stories -
seasonal offers

## Sales

Primary sales are recurring B2B relationships supported by dynamic
retail.

## Financial Model

Initial investment: €500,000

Allocation: - Cold storage €120k - Vehicle €55k - Equipment €35k -
Working capital €140k - Software €75k - Marketing €25k - Contingency
€50k

Revenue forecast: - 2027: €1.25M - 2028: €1.70M - 2029: €2.15M

Target inventory loss below 4%.

## Risks

-   Climate
-   Supply variability
-   Dependence on Thomas
-   Premium market cycles

Mitigation: - diversified suppliers - ML planning - adaptive pricing -
retail balancing
