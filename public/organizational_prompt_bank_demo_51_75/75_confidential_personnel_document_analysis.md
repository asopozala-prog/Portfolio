# 75 — Confidential personnel document analysis

**Executor Type:** `Local / Private AI`  
**Executor:** `Local LLM System`  
**Environment:** `Private Workspace`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Classification`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Confidential personnel document analysis`

---

## Scope
Analyze confidential personnel documents for the specific HR question provided.

## Extract
Use only the minimum relevant facts: employment dates, role history, contractual status, recorded actions, qualifications, or other explicitly documented HR information.

## Separate
Keep records, people, time periods, and document sources clearly distinct. Do not infer health, performance, intent, or protected characteristics.

## Output
Give a factual summary and list any source-supported HR issues requiring review.

## Privacy
Keep processing local. Do not expose unrelated personnel data, make employment decisions, or guess when records conflict.
