# 57 — Internal guideline consistency review

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Docs`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `People & HR`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Validation`, `Analysis`  
**Task:** `Internal guideline consistency review`

---

## Scope
Review internal guidelines for contradictions that could lead employees to act differently.

## Compare
Check terminology, responsibilities, approval paths, deadlines, exceptions, eligibility rules, and references between sections.

## Distinguish
Separate harmless wording variation from operational conflict. Note when one section appears newer but authority is not clear.

## Report
List each inconsistency with both passages, the practical consequence, and the clarification required.

## Rule
Do not rewrite the guidelines or choose which rule is correct unless the document itself establishes priority.
