# 59 — Employee onboarding presentation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Slides`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Transformation`, `Generation`  
**Task:** `Employee onboarding presentation`

---

## Assignment
Create a practical onboarding presentation from the supplied employee material.

## Priorities
Explain who the team is, what the new employee needs to do first, essential tools, key contacts, basic working routines, and where to find help.

## Sequence
Organize slides around the employee journey: Welcome, First Day, First Week, How We Work, Where to Get Help.

## Output
Give each slide a clear title, 3–5 short points, and any action the employee must take.

## Check
Do not invent benefits, access rights, contacts, or policies. Mark missing onboarding information for HR.
