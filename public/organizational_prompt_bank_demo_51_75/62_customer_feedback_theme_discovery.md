# 62 — Customer feedback theme discovery

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Files`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Customer feedback theme discovery`

---

## Objective
Discover meaningful themes across the supplied customer feedback files.

## Group
Cluster comments by underlying need, complaint, praise, request, friction point, or usage experience. Keep important minority viewpoints visible.

## Interpret
For each theme, note how often it appears relative to the dataset and whether the tone is mostly positive, negative, or mixed.

## Output
Return 5–8 themes with a short description, representative evidence, and business relevance.

## Caution
Do not claim the feedback represents all customers unless the dataset supports that conclusion.
