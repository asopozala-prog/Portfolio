# 65 — Long-form business report analysis

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`

**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Finance & Administration`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Summarization`, `Extraction`  
**Task:** `Long-form business report analysis`

---

## Purpose
Analyze a long business report for senior management and finance reviewers.

## Read For
Find the core thesis, major findings, financial evidence, assumptions, forecasts, recommendations, and the strongest risks or opportunities.

## Challenge
Identify where conclusions rely on weak evidence, selective metrics, unsupported forecasts, or inconsistent definitions.

## Output
Return: Executive View, Key Evidence, Financial Implications, Risks, Questions for Review.

## Accuracy
Preserve units, periods, and calculation context. Do not convert commentary into fact or simplify away conflicting evidence.
