# 56 — Customer complaint analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Gmail`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Classification`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Customer complaint analysis`

---

## Goal
Analyze a customer complaint thread before the team responds.

## Extract
Identify what happened, what the customer expected, what they received instead, prior contacts, relevant dates, and the remedy they are asking for.

## Diagnose
Separate product/service failure from communication failure. Classify the complaint by topic and apparent severity based on the correspondence.

## Output
Provide: Complaint Summary, Evidence, Customer Expectation, Escalation Need, Recommended Next Step.

## Limit
Do not admit fault, promise compensation, or assume policy entitlement. Highlight facts that still need checking.
