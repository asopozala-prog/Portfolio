# 64 — Multi-document requirement mapping

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`  
**Environment:** `Documents`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Comparison`, `Classification`  
**Task:** `Multi-document requirement mapping`

---

## Mission
Build a traceable map of requirements spread across several documents.

## Extract
Capture each obligation, deliverable, deadline, condition, approval, responsibility, and acceptance criterion together with its source section.

## Relate
Mark requirements as Unique, Duplicate, Complementary, Overlapping, or Conflicting. Keep similar-looking requirements separate unless they truly mean the same thing.

## Output
Create a requirement map with requirement text, source, responsible party if stated, and relationship to other requirements.

## Check
Flag vague wording and unresolved conflicts. Do not silently merge requirements.
