# 66 — Knowledge source contradiction review

**Executor Type:** `Claude Workspace`  
**Executor:** `Claude`  
**Environment:** `Knowledge Work`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Analysis`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Knowledge source contradiction review`

---

## Goal
Detect contradictions across the organization’s knowledge sources.

## Compare
Look for different answers to the same question about process, ownership, deadline, policy, status, definition, or required action.

## Diagnose
Classify each case as Direct Contradiction, Version Difference, Scope Difference, or Unclear. Use dates and document status where available.

## Output
Show both positions side by side, their sources, and what needs clarification.

## Governance
Do not choose a “correct” answer unless source authority clearly proves it. Preserve unresolved conflicts for an owner to resolve.
