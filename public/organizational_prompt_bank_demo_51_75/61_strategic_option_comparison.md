# 61 — Strategic option comparison

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Analysis`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Comparison`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Strategic option comparison`

---

## Decision
Compare the strategic options provided and identify the strongest choice under the stated business priorities.

## Evaluate
For each option, consider strategic fit, expected value, effort, risk, timing, dependencies, reversibility, and capability requirements where evidence exists.

## Trade-offs
Show what each option gains and sacrifices. Identify assumptions that could change the ranking.

## Output
Provide a compact option comparison and a recommendation with the conditions under which it holds.

## Discipline
Do not fabricate ROI, costs, or probabilities. If evidence is insufficient, state what must be known before deciding.
