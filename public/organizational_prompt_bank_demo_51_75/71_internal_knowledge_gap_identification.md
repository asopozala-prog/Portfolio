# 71 — Internal knowledge gap identification

**Executor Type:** `Productivity / Knowledge AI`  
**Executor:** `Notion AI`  
**Environment:** `Internal Knowledge`  
**Department Tags:** `Operations`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Classification`, `Research`  
**Task:** `Internal knowledge gap identification`

---

## Objective
Find gaps in the internal knowledge base that create repeated work or unreliable answers.

## Evidence
Look for recurring questions without answers, outdated instructions, contradictory pages, missing process steps, and topics that exist only in meeting notes or individual memory.

## Prioritize
Rank gaps by frequency, operational impact, and risk of inconsistent decisions.

## Output
For each gap provide: Missing Knowledge, Evidence, Affected Work, Suggested Knowledge Item.

## Distinction
Separate true knowledge gaps from poor searchability. Do not recommend creating duplicate documentation when usable content already exists.
