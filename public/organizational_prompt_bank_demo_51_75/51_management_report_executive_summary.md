# 51 — Management report executive summary

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Word`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Extraction`  
**Task:** `Management report executive summary`

---

## Purpose
Condense the selected management report for an executive who will not read the full document.

## What to Pull
Capture the main business result, the few figures that define performance, major changes versus plan, and any explicit recommendations.

## Executive Lens
Prioritize consequences for strategy, resources, risk, or leadership decisions. Ignore supporting detail unless it changes the conclusion.

## Deliverable
Write one short executive summary, then list: Key Results, Risks, Decisions Needed.

## Guardrail
Keep all numbers, periods, and claims faithful to the report. Mark unclear assumptions instead of smoothing them over.
