# 55 — Meeting risk and issue identification

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Teams`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Operations`  
**Function Tags:** `Extraction`, `Classification`, `Analysis`  
**Task:** `Meeting risk and issue identification`

---

## Task
Extract risks and active issues from the Teams meeting.

## Detect
Capture blockers, dependencies, resource problems, disputed assumptions, delivery threats, unresolved decisions, and issues already affecting work.

## Separate
Use these labels: Future Risk, Active Issue, Dependency, Decision Needed. Record owner and timing only when stated.

## Produce
Create a compact risk-and-issue register with evidence from the meeting and the agreed or proposed next step.

## Boundary
Do not turn brainstorming or casual concern into a formal risk. If severity or ownership is unclear, say so.
