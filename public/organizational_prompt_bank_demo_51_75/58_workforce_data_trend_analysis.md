# 58 — Workforce data trend analysis

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Google Gemini`  
**Environment:** `Google Sheets`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Workforce data trend analysis`

---

## Question
What workforce trends in this Google Sheet deserve HR or management attention?

## Measures
Use the available fields such as headcount, hires, exits, absence, tenure, department, location, or contract type. Compare periods and groups only when definitions are consistent.

## Look For
Identify sustained growth or decline, turnover changes, concentration, unusual absence patterns, and abrupt shifts.

## Output
Summarize the strongest trends with supporting figures and 2–4 management questions.

## Safety
Do not infer motivation, performance, health, or protected characteristics. Flag small samples and inconsistent HR data.
