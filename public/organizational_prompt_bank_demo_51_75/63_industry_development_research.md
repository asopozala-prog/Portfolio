# 63 — Industry development research

**Executor Type:** `General AI`  
**Executor:** `ChatGPT Workspace`  
**Environment:** `Research`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`, `Sales & Marketing`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Analysis`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Industry development research`

---

## Research Question
Identify recent industry developments that could materially affect the business.

## Investigate
Look across market structure, customer behavior, competitors, technology, regulation, pricing, and distribution. Prefer recent credible sources.

## Prioritize
Select developments that could change demand, economics, operating model, or competitive advantage—not general industry news.

## Output
For each development provide: What Changed, Evidence, Likely Business Relevance, Confidence.

## Source Rule
Include source dates and separate verified facts from your interpretation. Do not present speculation as an established trend.
