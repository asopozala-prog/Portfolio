# 54 — Strategy proposal presentation

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `PowerPoint`  
**Department Tags:** `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Transformation`, `Generation`, `Summarization`  
**Task:** `Strategy proposal presentation`

---

## Brief
Turn the strategy proposal into a management-ready presentation.

## Storyline
Build the argument in this order: strategic challenge, proposed direction, evidence, expected business value, major dependencies, risks, decision requested.

## Slide Discipline
Use one message per slide. Replace dense prose with short evidence points. Keep optional background out of the main narrative.

## Output
Create a slide-by-slide outline with title, key message, and supporting evidence.

## Integrity
Do not strengthen the proposal with invented market data, savings, or forecasts. Mark any claim that needs evidence before presentation.
