# 52 — Cash-flow trend review

**Executor Type:** `Workplace AI`  
**Executor:** `Microsoft 365 Copilot`  
**Environment:** `Excel`  
**Department Tags:** `Finance & Administration`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Analysis`, `Comparison`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Cash-flow trend review`

---

## Objective
Review the cash-flow sheet for signs of liquidity strength, pressure, or unusual movement.

## Examine
Compare inflows and outflows by period. Look for sharp changes in operating cash, financing activity, investment outflows, and ending cash where those fields exist.

## Interpret
Call out recurring negative cash movement, one-off spikes, delayed receipts, or unusually high payments only when visible in the data.

## Result
Summarize the overall direction, the 3–5 most important changes, and what management should monitor next.

## Validation
Check totals and periods before commenting. Do not invent causes for movements that the workbook does not explain.
