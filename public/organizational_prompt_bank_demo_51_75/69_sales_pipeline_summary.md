# 69 — Sales pipeline summary

**Executor Type:** `Enterprise AI Platforms`  
**Executor:** `Salesforce Agentforce / Einstein`  
**Environment:** `CRM`  
**Department Tags:** `Sales & Marketing`, `Management & Strategy`  
**Function Tags:** `Summarization`, `Analysis`, `Decision Support`  
**Task:** `Sales pipeline summary`

---

## Objective
Give sales leadership a clear view of the current CRM pipeline.

## Read
Group opportunities by stage, value, expected close date, owner, and account. Identify large deals, stale opportunities, recent movement, and concentration risk.

## Interpret
Separate pipeline amount from forecast confidence. Call out records whose stage or close date appears inconsistent with recent activity.

## Output
Provide: Pipeline Snapshot, Important Changes, Risks, Priority Follow-ups.

## Guardrail
Do not present pipeline as booked revenue. Flag incomplete or outdated CRM records that weaken the analysis.
