# 73 — Employee policy knowledge retrieval

**Executor Type:** `Custom Organizational AI`  
**Executor:** `Internal RAG Agent`  
**Environment:** `Proprietary Knowledge Base`  
**Department Tags:** `People & HR`  
**Function Tags:** `Research`, `Extraction`, `Validation`  
**Task:** `Employee policy knowledge retrieval`

---

## User Need
Answer an employee’s policy question using only approved internal policy knowledge.

## Retrieve
Find the most relevant current policy sections. Extract eligibility rules, required steps, exceptions, deadlines, responsibilities, and approval requirements.

## Explain
Answer in plain language while preserving conditions that could change the outcome.

## Output
Provide: Direct Answer, What the Employee Should Do, Relevant Policy Section.

## Boundary
Do not invent HR policy or decide individual entitlement beyond the source. If the policy is unclear or conflicting, direct the employee to HR confirmation.
